# Changelog

## 2.0.0 — pending VCV Library acceptance

First proprietary freeware release candidate. Not yet submitted to the VCV
Library.

- Dattorro figure-of-eight plate reverb, implemented from the published
  algorithm (JAES 45(9), 1997)
- Two-voice pitch shifter as a moving read of the tank delays, −24 to +24
  semitones each, with 1 V/oct control over voice A
- **ONSET and RISE** — dead time before shifted content enters, and how
  gradually it swells in once it does. One bloom per phrase
- Freeze with pitch lock, so a held tail keeps the pitch it was frozen at
- Size changeable while audio is running
- Selectable classic Chladni and organic botanical displays, driven by the
  controls and audio
- Full CV with attenuverters, freeze gate, and dual-mode bypass
- Five factory presets and final 20 HP botanical panel artwork
